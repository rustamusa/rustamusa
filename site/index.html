What a first day looks like

I come to you, and you pick the problem. The goal for day one: something works by evening that didn't work in the morning.

  1. Morning

    I watch the flow

    How does a call, an email or a walk-in turn into money? I follow one real order from start to finish and write down where it stalls.

  2. Midday

    You pick one thing

    I show you what I saw. You choose the problem worth fixing first. It's your business, so it's your call.

  3. Afternoon

    We build it together

    On your accounts, with your real examples, with you or your staff at the keyboard.

  4. End of day

    You run it

    You do it yourself while I watch. I leave a one-page how-to, and I'm a text away after that.

Elevator cab maker in New York. Owner: Steven. I draw the approval sets a building signs before a new cab interior is built.

The problem

Every new cab needs a set the building signs: plans, details and a weight calculation that all have to agree. A set took about a week, and the drafter was far away.

What I did

I draw the whole set with AI tools from one 3D model, so the renders, elevations, details and the weight calculation all agree with each other.

What changed

A week became a couple of hours.

Changes go back the same day. Four revisions went out between November 5 and November 10.

Renders and drawings from the same model

The perspectives on sheet 1 come from the model that makes the plans and details, so what the building approves is what gets built.

What's in the set

  • Renders and parts listRear, corner and landing views, and every part with its material and weight.
  • Plans and elevationsPlan view, ceiling plan, all walls, the door wall and canopy, ventilation area.
  • DetailsPanel joints, strike jamb, return, handrail, base, ceiling and access panel.
  • Weight calculationHow each weight in the parts list is made, adding up to 553 lb.
  • Care sheet for the buildingCleaning instructions, a maintenance schedule and the material key.
  • Local and fastThe drafter used to be far away. I'm in Brooklyn and turn changes around the same day.
