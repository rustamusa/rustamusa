How a first day goes

The owner picks the problem. The goal: by evening something works that didn't work in the morning.

  1. Morning

    Watch the flow

    How does a call, an email or a walk-in turn into money? One real order followed start to finish. Every stall written down.

  2. Midday

    The owner picks one thing

    The findings go on the table. The owner chooses what to fix first.

  3. Afternoon

    Built together

    On the business's own accounts, with real examples, the owner or staff at the keyboard.

  4. End of day

    The team runs it

    Staff do it themselves, watched once. A one-page how-to stays behind. After that, help is one text away.

One rule

Only what moves money or saves time gets touched: the quote, the order, the booking, the reply. The rest of the business stays as built.

The approval sets a building signs before a new cab interior is built.

Problem

Every new cab needs a set the building signs: plans, details and a weight calculation that all have to agree. A set took about a week, and the drafter was far away.

Fix

The whole set is drawn with AI tools from one 3D model, so the renders, elevations, details and the weight calculation agree with each other.

Result

Hours.

A set takes a couple of hours, not a week. Changes go back the same day. Four revisions went out between November 5 and November 10.

143Dimensions checked
553 lbCab weight, calculated part by part
Same dayTurnaround on a change

Renders and drawings from the same model

The perspectives on sheet 1 come from the model that makes the plans and details, so what the building approves is what gets built.

What's in the set

  • Renders and parts listRear, corner and landing views, and every part with its material and weight.
  • Plans and elevationsPlan view, ceiling plan, all walls, the door wall and canopy, ventilation area.
  • DetailsPanel joints, strike jamb, return, handrail, base, ceiling and access panel.
  • Weight calculationHow each weight in the parts list is made, adding up to 553 lb.
  • Care sheet for the buildingCleaning instructions, a maintenance schedule and the material key.
  • Local and fastThe drafter used to be far away. Now the drawings are made in the same building as the shop.

All ten sheets of revision 3. The building address is removed from every title block.
